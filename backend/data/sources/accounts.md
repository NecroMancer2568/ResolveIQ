# NovaMart Account & Security FAQ
Version: 3.0 | Effective: 2026-08-05 | Owner: Account Security

## Password Reset
Customers can initiate a password reset using their registered email address. Reset links are confidential and should not be forwarded.

## Locked Account
An account may be temporarily locked after repeated unsuccessful authentication attempts. Use the approved account recovery procedure rather than bypassing security controls.

## Suspicious Login
For an unfamiliar login, recommend changing the password through the official recovery flow and reviewing recent account activity. Escalate suspected compromise to Account Security.

## Personal Information
Never request passwords, authentication codes, full payment card numbers, or unnecessary secrets through support chat.

## Identity Verification
Additional verification may be required before changing sensitive account information. Follow the approved verification procedure.
