# NovaMart Refund & Return Policy
Version: 3.2 | Effective: 2026-08-01 | Owner: Customer Operations

## Standard Refund Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of delivery for eligible physical products. Products should normally be unused and returned with original packaging and included accessories.

## Damaged or Incorrect Items
If an item arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from the order, report the issue within 7 days of delivery. Customer Operations may request photographs or other evidence before approving a replacement or refund.

## Refund Processing
After an approved return is received and inspected, the refund is normally initiated within 5 business days. The bank or card provider may require additional time before the credit appears.

## Exceptions
Digital products are non-refundable after download or activation except where required by applicable law. Gift cards are non-refundable except where required by applicable law.

## Escalation
If a refund was approved but has not appeared after the stated processing period plus normal banking delay, escalate to Payments.
