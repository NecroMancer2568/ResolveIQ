# NovaMart Product Warranty Policy
Version: 1.9 | Effective: 2026-06-10 | Owner: Product Support

## Warranty Coverage
Eligible products include a limited warranty against manufacturing defects for the period specified on the product page or warranty record.

## Exclusions
Normal wear, accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized modification, and damage caused by incompatible accessories may be excluded.

## Warranty Claim
Customers should provide the order number and a description of the problem. Photos or diagnostic information may be requested when appropriate.

## Replacement or Repair
After eligibility is confirmed, Product Support determines whether repair, replacement, or another approved remedy is appropriate.

## Escalation
If warranty eligibility is unclear or the product page and warranty record disagree, do not make a definitive promise. Escalate the case for review.
