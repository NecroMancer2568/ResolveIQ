# NovaMart Shipping & Delivery Policy
Version: 4.1 | Effective: 2026-08-15 | Owner: Fulfillment Operations

## Standard Delivery
Standard domestic delivery is normally 3–7 business days after dispatch. The checkout date is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date.

## Delayed Packages
If tracking has not updated for 72 hours, verify the carrier status. If the package is still moving, provide the latest estimated delivery information.

## Lost Packages
Treat a package as potentially lost when the carrier reports it lost or its investigation threshold has been reached. Do not promise a refund before the required investigation is complete.

## Incorrect Address
If an order has not shipped, Operations may be able to update the address. After carrier handoff, changes depend on carrier capabilities.

## Damaged Delivery
Customers should report visible shipping damage as soon as possible, preferably within 7 days, with photographs when available.
