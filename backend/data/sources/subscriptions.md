# NovaMart Subscription & Cancellation Policy
Version: 2.4 | Effective: 2026-09-01 | Owner: Subscription Operations

## Cancellation
Customers can request cancellation at any time. Unless plan terms state otherwise, cancellation stops the next renewal but does not automatically refund a period already started.

## Renewal
Renewals are charged according to the plan billing interval. Customers should review the renewal date displayed in their account.

## Accidental Renewal
For an unexpected renewal, verify the transaction and applicable plan terms before promising a refund. Eligible exceptions should be reviewed by Subscription Operations.

## Plan Changes
Upgrades or downgrades may take effect immediately or at the next billing cycle depending on the plan. Verify the current plan before describing the financial effect.

## Escalation
Billing disputes involving subscription renewals should be escalated when the applicable plan terms do not clearly determine the outcome.
